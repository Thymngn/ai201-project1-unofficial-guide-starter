OVERVIEW
---------
The Boca Raton campus is home to more than 4,000 on-campus residents in nine apartment and
suite-style residence halls. The university delivers residence hall-based programming to help
students develop skills for lifelong learning.

Housing contact:
  Email: housing@fau.edu
  Phone: 561-297-2880
  Website: fau.edu/housing

BOCA RATON CAMPUS — RESIDENCE HALLS
======================================

1. ALGONQUIN HALL
   - Suite-style (mix of double and single rooms)
   - Amenities: Study lounge, computer lab, multipurpose room with large-screen TV,
     laundry facilities, cable TV, ice machine, mail room services, WiFi, BBQ grills,
     sand volleyball court

2. ATLANTIC PARK TOWERS (APT)
   - Suite-style (double and single rooms) 
   - first-year students
   - Houses the Resident Learning Community
   - Amenities: 24-hour desk, study lounges, multipurpose room with TV, complimentary
     laundry facilities, cable TV, ice machine, mailroom, computer lab, community kitchen,
     BBQ grills, WiFi

3. GLADES PARK TOWERS (GPT)
   - Suite-style (double and single rooms)
   - Houses the Resident Learning Community
   - Amenities: 24-hour desk, study lounges, multipurpose room with TV, complimentary
     laundry facilities, cable TV, ice machine, mailroom, computer lab, community kitchen,
     BBQ grills, WiFi

4. HERITAGE PARK TOWERS (HPT)
   - Suite-style for first-year students
   - Amenities: 24-hour desk, study lounges, community features

5. INDIAN RIVER TOWERS (IRT)
   - Suite-style for upperclassmen (double and single rooms)
   - Amenities: 24-hour desk, study lounges, TV lounge, laundry, cable TV, ice machine,
     mailroom, computer lab, WiFi, community kitchen, BBQ grills

6. INNOVATION VILLAGE APARTMENTS NORTH (IVA North)
   - Upperclassmen residence hall
   - 4-bedroom, 2-bathroom apartment units
   - Amenities: 24-hour desk, conference rooms, study lounges, multipurpose room with
     large-screen TV, cable TV, ice machine, mailroom, BBQ grills, sand volleyball court,
     community swimming pool, WiFi

7. INNOVATION VILLAGE APARTMENTS SOUTH (IVA South)
   - Upperclassmen
   - 4-bedroom apartment units
   - Amenities: Conference rooms, study lounges

8. TALON HALL (NEW — Opening Fall 2026)
   - Upperclassmen residence hall
   - Room types:
       • One Bedroom Single Suite: $5,834/student/semester
       • Three Bedroom Single Room Suite: $5,500/student/semester
   - Amenities: 24-hour front desk, package lockers, furniture & blinds, resident-only
     access, WiFi, vending machines, study areas, community kitchens, free laundry,
     sun deck, outdoor pavilion, BBQ grill, fire pit, game room
   - Bed size: Extra-long twin (36" x 80")

JUPITER CAMPUS — RESIDENCE HALLS (WILKES HONORS COLLEGE)
===========================================================
Contact: jupiterhousing@fau.edu | 561-297-2880

Residence Hall 1 & 2:
   - For first-year AND returning students
   - 4-person suites (4 single bedrooms + shared living room) OR 1-bedroom double with 1 bath
   - Amenities: Fully furnished, WiFi, free laundry, vending machines, BBQ pits

Residence Hall 3:
   - For returning upperclassmen
   - 4-person and 2-person single suites
   - Amenities: 24-hour desk, fully furnished, WiFi, free laundry, fitness center,
     community kitchenettes, community lounge, package & mail center

2026–2027 Jupiter Rates (per student, per semester):
   - Suite with two single rooms: $5,684
   - Meal plans required for all Jupiter campus residents

HOUSING REQUIREMENTS
---------------------
- First-time-in-college (FTIC) students at Harriet L. Wilkes Honors College are required
  to live on campus for their first two years and purchase a meal plan.
- Meal plans are required for students in APT, PAR, IRT, HPT, and GPT residence halls.
- Students in IVA and UVA are NOT required to have a residential meal plan (optional).
