FLORIDA ATLANTIC UNIVERSITY — CAMPUS SERVICES
==============================================
Source: https://www.fau.edu/shs/ | https://www.fau.edu/counseling/ | https://www.fau.edu/about/campus-life

OVERVIEW
--------
FAU provides a comprehensive array of campus services to support student academic success,
health, safety, and overall wellbeing through the Division of Student Affairs & Enrollment
Management.

STUDENT HEALTH SERVICES (SHS)
-------------------------------
- Location: Boca Raton campus — 2nd floor of the Breezeway (above Starbucks) [as of Aug 2026
  after renovation; temporarily at Heritage Park Towers during summer 2026]
- Also available: Jupiter and Davie campus clinics
- Phone: 561-297-3512
- Services offered:
    • Primary & Acute Health Care
    • Sexual & Reproductive Health
    • Nutrition Services
    • Psychiatric Services
    • Flu Clinic
    • Public Health Advisory
    • Faculty and Staff services
- Appointments: In-person or telehealth; schedule online or by phone
- Note: As of April 17, 2026, the SHS Dental Clinic has permanently closed.

COUNSELING AND PSYCHOLOGICAL SERVICES (CAPS)
---------------------------------------------
- Phone: 561-297-CAPS (2277)
- Services:
    • Individual counseling
    • Group counseling (Process Groups, Support Groups, Skill-Based Groups)
    • Psychiatric services
    • Relationship counseling
    • Outreach and prevention education
    • Crisis/emergency services
    • Let's Talk (drop-in consultations)
    • Counselors in Residence
- Digital tools: Welltrack Boost (free on-demand digital self-help tools for all students)
- CAPS is a confidential service.

OWLS CARE HEALTH PROMOTION
----------------------------
- Phone: 561-297-1048
- Award-winning peer education and health promotion programs
- Part of the campus mental health network

DEAN OF STUDENTS OFFICE
-------------------------
- Phone: 561-297-3542

    • Victim Services
    • Case management
    • Homelessness verification

    • Other student concerns and resources

STUDENT ACCESSIBILITY SERVICES (SAS)
--------------------------------------

NIGHTOWLS ESCORT SERVICE
-------------------------
- Student-run evening escort service (free)
- Available to FAU students needing a safe ride to/from class, library, residence halls,
  parking lots, or any other campus location at Boca Raton
