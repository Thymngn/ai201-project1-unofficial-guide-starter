OVERVIEW
---------
FAU Parking and Transportation Services provides a variety of campus transportation and
parking services across all FAU campuses. The office is located in the Student Services
Building (SU-80) on the Boca Raton campus.

Contact: faupark@fau.edu
Website: https://www.fau.edu/parking/

ON-CAMPUS SHUTTLE (BOCA RATON)
================================
- Free shuttle bus operating throughout the Boca Raton campus
- Available to students, faculty, and staff
- Serves remote parking areas and student apartments
- Hours: Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM
- Requirement: Must show FAU OWL Card to board
- Real-time tracking: Download the FAU Mobile App to track shuttle location

BOCA–JUPITER INTERCAMPUS SHUTTLE
==================================
- Free shuttle between the Boca Raton campus and the Jupiter (John D. MacArthur) campus
- Route: Administration Building (AD 10) on Boca campus ↔ Jupiter Research Facility (MC 17 & 19)
- Operates Monday–Friday
- Shuttles equipped with wireless Internet
- For schedule: https://www.fau.edu/parking/bocajupitershuttle/

NIGHTOWLS ESCORT SERVICE
=========================
- Student-run evening escort service
- Free rides for FAU students to/from class, library, residence halls, parking lots,
  or any other location on the Boca Raton campus
- Available during evening hours

PALM TRAN (PUBLIC BUS)
=======================
- Palm Tran Route 94 runs between the Yamato Tri-Rail Station and the FAU Administration
  Building parking lot
- Frequency: Every 20 minutes, 6:45 AM – 9:00 PM
- Travel time: ~10 minutes
- Cost: Free for FAU students/staff when showing FAU OWL Card
- Route schedules available at the Student Support Services Building on campus
- Website: https://www.fau.edu/parking/palmtran/

TRI-RAIL (COMMUTER TRAIN)
==========================
- Runs North–South between Miami-Dade County and Palm Beach County
- Connects to FAU via the Yamato Tri-Rail Station (then take Palm Tran Route 94)
- Student Discount Program: 50% off Tri-Rail fare with proof of enrollment
  (show FAU OWL Card and/or class schedule)
- Avoid traffic and parking by using Tri-Rail

PARKING PERMITS
================
- Students pay for parking permits through tuition and fees
- Permits are renewed each year in August (reminder email sent via myFAU)
- Purchase/renew permits through myFAU
- Be aware of parking rules and regulations for your permit color zone
- 3+ unpaid citations = vehicle boot
- License plate number serves as the vehicle identifier on FAU's system
- Green parking is for dorm residence, Blue parking is for commuters, and Red parking is for reserved with specific permission

SPECIAL PARKING SERVICES
==========================
- Carpool Program: Carpool parking spaces available around campus
  (https://www.fau.edu/parking/carpool/)
- Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging: Multiple EV charging stations across campuses
  (https://www.fau.edu/parking/evchargers/)
- FAU Parking App: Find parking in real time
  (https://www.fau.edu/parking/apps/)
- Visitor Parking: Options available depending on length of visit

FORT LAUDERDALE CAMPUS PARKING
================================
- Students can park on the 6th and 7th floors of the parking garage (144 SE 1st Ave.)
  in areas dedicated to FAU/Broward College (floors 7, 6A, 6C, and ramp 6E)
- Hangtag required; pick up in the lobby of the Ft. Lauderdale campus (111 East Las Olas Blvd.)
- Flat lot access: Monday–Friday 6 PM–5 AM, and all weekend
