FLORIDA ATLANTIC UNIVERSITY — COLLEGES
========================================
Source: https://www.fau.edu/provost/about/colleges/
        https://www.flbog.edu/university/florida-atlantic-university/
        https://www.fau.edu/about/academics/

FAU operates 10 academic colleges:

1. DOROTHY F. SCHMIDT COLLEGE OF ARTS AND LETTERS
---------------------------------------------------
- Offers programs in humanities, social sciences, and communication
- Includes schools such as: School of Communication and Multimedia Studies, School of the Arts,
  and departments in History, English, Languages, Philosophy, and more
- Students in the Honors College choose concentrations from the humanities, social sciences,
  and natural sciences
- Clubs: Film Club, Coastlines (literary magazine), Dance clubs, Lambda Pi Eta, and more

2. COLLEGE OF BUSINESS (BARRY KAYE COLLEGE OF BUSINESS)
---------------------------------------------------------
- Fully accredited (AACSB)
- Entrepreneurship Program ranked in the top 50 in the U.S. by Success Magazine and best in
  Florida for entrepreneurship
- Department of Information Technology and Operations Management ranked in national top 50 by
  Decision Line
- Offers undergraduate and graduate business degrees

3. COLLEGE OF SOCIAL WORK AND CRIMINAL JUSTICE
------------------------------------------------
- Offers programs in social work and criminal justice at undergraduate and graduate levels
- Strong community partnerships and focus on social impact

4. COLLEGE OF EDUCATION
------------------------
- One of FAU's original colleges (opened 1964)
- Offers education degree programs at the undergraduate and graduate levels
- Elementary Education is one of the top undergraduate majors by enrollment
- Partners with A.D. Henderson School (K-8 laboratory school on FAU campus) and FAU High School
- Henderson University School is rated an "A+ School" by the Florida Department of Education

5. COLLEGE OF ENGINEERING AND COMPUTER SCIENCE
------------------------------------------------
- Offers undergraduate and graduate degrees in engineering, computer science, and applied sciences
- Home to the nation's FIRST undergraduate Ocean Engineering program
- Programs accredited by ABET (Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering)
- Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science (EECS) recently launched:
    • BA in Computer Science
    • BS and MS in Data Science and Analytics (DSA)
    • MS in Artificial Intelligence (AI)

6. THE GRADUATE COLLEGE
------------------------
- Oversees graduate education across all disciplines
- Coordinates Ph.D., Master's, and graduate certificate programs university-wide

7. HARRIET L. WILKES HONORS COLLEGE
-------------------------------------
- First public honors college built from the ground up in the United States (opened Fall 1999)
- Located on the Jupiter campus
- All students complete either an internship OR a study abroad
- Internship access at Scripps Florida, Max Planck Florida Institute, and more
- All students write an Honors Thesis in their senior year
- The Wilkes Medical Scholars Program allows select students to earn a B.A. from Wilkes Honors
  College and an M.D. from FAU's College of Medicine in 7–8 years
- First-time-in-college (FTIC) students required to live on campus for their first two years

8. CHARLES E. SCHMIDT COLLEGE OF MEDICINE
-------------------------------------------
- Authorized to award the M.D. degree by the Florida Board of Governors on April 7, 2010
- Received preliminary accreditation from LCME in February 2011
- Collaborates with Scripps Florida, Max Planck Florida Institute, Torrey Pines Institute

9. CHRISTINE E. LYNN COLLEGE OF NURSING
-----------------------------------------
- Offers nursing education at undergraduate and graduate levels (BSN, MSN, DNP, Ph.D.)
- Financial aid and scholarships available specifically for nursing students
- Strong focus on community health and clinical practice

10. CHARLES E. SCHMIDT COLLEGE OF SCIENCE
-------------------------------------------
- Offers BA and BS degrees in Biology, Chemistry, Exercise Science, Geosciences, Math,
  Physics, and Psychology
- Graduate programs: Ph.D. and Master's in Biology, Chemistry & Biochemistry, Neuroscience,
  Psychology, Geosciences, Mathematics, Environmental Science, and Physics
- Pre-Health Professions Office supports students pursuing medicine, dentistry, veterinary
  medicine, PA, pharmacy, optometry, and other health careers
- Accelerated BS+MS 5-year programs available in Biology and Mathematics
- Collaborations with Scripps Florida, Max Planck Florida Institute, Torrey Pines Institute
