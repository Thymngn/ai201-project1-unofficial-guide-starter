FLORIDA ATLANTIC UNIVERSITY — ADMISSIONS (GENERAL)
====================================================
Source: https://www.fau.edu/admissions/freshman/how-to-apply/admissions-requirements/
        https://www.fau.edu/registrar/university-catalog/catalog/admissions/
        https://www.usnews.com/best-colleges/florida-atlantic-university-1481/applying

OVERVIEW
--------
Florida Atlantic University admissions is moderately selective. Each year, FAU receives more
applications than allotted freshman seats for the incoming fall class. Meeting minimum eligibility
requirements set by the Florida Board of Governors does NOT guarantee admission.

Acceptance Rate: ~66–73% (moderately selective)

APPLICATION BASICS
-------------------
- Application Platform: Common App (online)
- Application Fee: $30 (non-refundable) for undergraduates
- Admissions Type: Rolling admissions (priority deadline: March 14 for fall)
- Enrollment Deposit: $200 non-refundable deposit required upon acceptance
- Orientation: Incoming freshmen must attend a S.O.A.R. (freshman orientation) session

TEST SCORES (FRESHMAN)
-----------------------
- SAT middle 50%: 1040–1200
- ACT middle 50%: 21–26
- Florida Board of Governors regulation requires first-year students to submit an ACT, SAT, or
  CLT score. Students who do not submit scores will NOT be eligible for merit scholarships.
- Note (as of 2025–26 cycle): ACT Science subsection is no longer required.

ACADEMIC REQUIREMENTS
----------------------
- GPA is a "very important" factor in admissions decisions.
- High school class rank (when available) is considered "very important."
- Letters of recommendation are NOT considered.
- AP credits are accepted; credit will not be awarded for both AP and CLEP or AP and Dual
  Enrollment covering the same course material.

INTERNATIONAL STUDENTS
-----------------------
- Must meet IELTS/TOEFL English proficiency requirements.
- Can submit GMAT or GRE scores for some graduate programs.
- FAU offers over 110 UG and PG-level programs for international students.

TRANSFER STUDENTS
------------------
- Students transferring with an Associate of Arts (A.A.) degree from a Florida College System
  institution must submit a final official transcript showing the A.A. before enrolling.
- FAU recommends reviewing academic program prerequisites before transferring.
