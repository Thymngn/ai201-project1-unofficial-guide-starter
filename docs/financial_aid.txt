OVERVIEW
---------
The Office of Student Financial Aid at FAU offers a comprehensive financial aid program to help
meet the financial needs of all students. FAU uses the FAFSA as the primary method to determine
eligibility for need-based aid.

- 91% of FAU students receive some form of financial aid
- Average total aid awarded per year: ~$10,857
- 57% of first-year students receive need-based financial aid
- Average net price for federal loan recipients: $8,636

FAFSA
------
- Complete the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) to be considered for grants,
  loans, and work-study
- FAU FAFSA School Code: (available at fau.edu/finaid)
- Log on to myFAU to review your status, outstanding requirements, and financial aid offer

TYPES OF AID
=============

GRANTS (do not need to be repaid)
-----------------------------------
1. Federal Pell Grant
   - For undergraduate students demonstrating financial need
   - Average Pell Grant at FAU: ~$7,590
   - Automatically accepted by FAU; apply via FAFSA
   - Not available to students who already hold a bachelor's or professional degree

2. Florida Resident Access Grant (FRAG)
   - State-run grant for Florida high school graduates attending public Florida institutions
   - Award: $200–$2,600 per academic year
   - For first-degree undergraduates

3. Benacquisto Scholarship Program
   - Merit-based scholarship for Florida high school graduates meeting specific criteria

SCHOLARSHIPS
=============
FAU uses Scholarship Universe — an online platform where students can apply for various internal
and external scholarships.

INSTITUTIONAL SCHOLARSHIPS
----------------------------
- Spirit of Atlantic Scholarship: 4-year merit-based scholarship for full-time enrollment
  students; disbursed every semester
- FAU 100 Scholarship: Similar to Spirit of Atlantic; Honors College students not eligible
- Ernest and Thea Kahn Endowed Scholarship Fund: For students who attended Palm Beach County
  high schools
- FAU Club / Kay S. Lioy Scholarship: For FAU employees, spouses, or children with minimum
  GPA of 2.0

STATE SCHOLARSHIPS
-------------------
- Florida Bright Futures Scholarship: Performance-based state scholarship for Florida high
  school graduates; can cover up to 100% of educational expenses at Florida Academic Scholars
  level
- Note: Submitting SAT/ACT/CLT scores is required to be eligible for merit scholarships from
  the Office of Undergraduate Admissions

FEDERAL AID BREAKDOWN (per Niche)
-----------------------------------
- Federal Grant Aid: 33% of students receive it; average $7,728
- State Grant Aid: 52% of students receive it; average $7,530
- Institution Grant Aid: 79% of students receive it; average $3,625

LOANS
------
- Federal Direct Loans (subsidized and unsubsidized) available via FAFSA
- Graduate students may be eligible for Direct PLUS Loans
- Doctoral students eligible for fellowships and scholarship grants

WORK-STUDY
-----------
- Need-Based Employment / Federal Work-Study available for eligible students
- Community Part-Time Jobs also listed through Financial Aid office

EMERGENCY FUNDING
------------------
- Florida Atlantic CARES Emergency Funding: Available for students experiencing financial hardship
- Apply through the Financial Aid office

IMPORTANT TIPS
---------------
- Rolling admissions: Apply early to maximize financial aid opportunities
- Use FAU's Net Price Calculator to estimate your out-of-pocket costs
- Visit Scholarship Universe through the FAU portal for scholarships
- Students can change/update their aid by logging into myFAU
