UNDERGRADUATE PROGRAMS
------------------------
1. Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (BSCS)
   - Intensive study of computers, software, and computational systems
   - Includes higher-level math and science requirements
   - Areas: Internet/web programming, mobile development, data mining & machine learning,
     AI, database systems, software engineering, theory of computing, video game development,
     human-computer interaction, social networks

2. Bachelor of Arts in Computer Science (BACS)
   - Focuses on design and development of computer software
   - General education-level math/science requirements (less intensive than BSCS)
   - Suitable for students interested in CS concepts with broader interdisciplinary goals

3. BS in Data Science and Analytics (DSA) — NEW

GRADUATE PROGRAMS
------------------
1. Master of Science in Computer Science (MSCS)
   - Thesis and Non-Thesis options (minimum 30 credits each)
   - Required breadth courses (one from each category):
       • Theory & Algorithms (COT prefix)
       • Software & Programming (COP or CEN prefix)
       • Systems & Applications (CAP, CIS, CNT, or CDA prefix)
   - Non-thesis: 7 elective courses
   - Thesis: 6 thesis credits + 5 elective courses
   - All students complete one semester of CGS 5937 Graduate Seminar (0 credits)
   - Career outcomes: Software Engineer, Software Architect, Big Data Engineer, Data Analytics
     Engineer, Database Engineer, Mobile Application Engineer, Cybersecurity Engineer,
     Senior Application Developer

2. MS in Data Science and Analytics (DSA) — NEW

3. MS in Artificial Intelligence (AI) — NEW
