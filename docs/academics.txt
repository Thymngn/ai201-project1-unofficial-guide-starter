OVERVIEW
--------
Florida Atlantic University offers a transformative education rooted in the belief that creativity
and dedication fuel academic excellence. The university offers more than 170 degree programs,
spanning undergraduate, graduate, and doctoral levels, including Certificate, Associate,
Bachelor's, Master's, and Doctoral degrees.

FAU is ranked #183 in National Universities (U.S. News & World Report, 2026 edition) and #30
in Top Performers on Social Mobility.

- Bachelor of Arts (BA) / Bachelor of Science (BS)
- Master's
- Post-Master's Certificate
- Doctoral (Ph.D., M.D.)

UNDERGRADUATE FACTS

- 69 distinct undergraduate degrees across 61 majors in 23 broad fields of study
- Total undergraduate enrollment: 24,942 (Fall 2024)
- Student-to-faculty ratio: 24:1
- 38.6% of classes have fewer than 20 students
- Average freshman retention rate: 82%
- 4-year graduation rate: 50%

HONORS PROGRAMS
- University Honors Program (2-year, 14 honors credits for incoming freshmen)
- Harriet L. Wilkes Honors College (public honors college, first of its kind in the nation)
- National Merit Scholars Program
- Honors-in-the-Major Programs (for qualified upper-division students)

--------------------------------
SPECIAL ACADEMIC OPPORTUNITIES
- Undergraduate research: FAU students participate in more than 6,000 undergraduate research
  experiences each year; ranked among the best in the country for undergraduate research.
- Study Abroad: Opportunities include the Galápagos Islands, Iceland, Italy, and many more.
- Online Programs: Nationally recognized for affordability and innovation.
- Continuing Education / Osher Lifelong Learning Institute
- FAU Libraries: Rich academic collections supporting the university curriculum.
