OVERVIEW
--------
FAU is home to more than 300–400 Registered Student Organizations (RSOs). Each year, more
than 5,000 student-led events create a lively and enriching campus experience. All RSOs are
student-run and listed in OwlCentral, FAU's student organization database.

HOW TO FIND ORGANIZATIONS
---------------------------
- OwlCentral: FAU's online directory of all registered student organizations
  (owl central accessible through the FAU website)
- Students can search by interest, category, or keyword
- Instagram: Further details about organization activity

STARTING A NEW ORGANIZATION
-----------------------------
1. Submit a Statement of Intent to Organize form (to involvement@fau.edu)
2. Receive "Interest Group" status
3. Have 6 weeks to recruit members and advertise
4. Submit to involvement@fau.edu:
     - Constitution (see guidelines at fau.edu/getinvolved)
     - Roster of at least 10 current FAU students
     - Optional: Organization logo
5. Complete Online Student Organization Training (via Blackboard)
6. Assign a full-time FAU faculty or staff advisor
7. Upon completion → elevated to Registered Student Organization (RSO) status

RSO RESPONSIBILITIES
---------------------
- Remain in good standing with FAU (Student Union, Student Government, departments)
- Adhere to non-discrimination and anti-hazing requirements
- Meet all fiscal obligations and abide by SG Accounting & Budgeting procedures
- Ensure proper event planning and execution
- Provide officer transition training
- Maintain communication with the organization advisor
