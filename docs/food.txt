OVERVIEW
---------
FAU Dining Services features 13 on-campus dining locations at the Boca Raton campus alone.
Both the Boca Raton and Jupiter campuses offer dining halls, cafes, and convenience stores.
The university works with many partners to provide varied, healthy, affordable, and
sustainable dining options for students, faculty, staff, and guests.

ON-CAMPUS DINING LOCATIONS (BOCA RATON)
=========================================
- Atlantic Dining Hall (all-you-can-eat buffet / all-day dining hall — main dining facility)
- Breezeway Food Court (central social hub; multiple dining options)
- Chick-fil-A
- Starbucks (in the Breezeway, above which is Student Health Services)
- Panda Express
- Einstein Bros. Bagels
- Outtakes Convenience Stores (multiple locations)
- Marketplace (in the Student Union)
- Wild Blue (retail dining)
- And more on-campus locations

MEAL PLANS
===========

RESIDENTIAL MEAL PLANS (required for most on-campus residents)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Students living in APT, PAR, IRT, HPT, and GPT are REQUIRED to have a meal plan.
Students in IVA (Innovation Village Apartments) and UVA may choose residential or commuter plans.

Plan Options (per semester):
  1. 19 Meals/Week
     - Price: $2,248.50/semester
     - Best for students with a full, busy schedule

  2. 15 Meals/Week + $100 Flex Bucks
     - Price: $2,123.50/semester
     - Balanced plan: structured meals + flexible campus dining spending

  3. 12 Meals/Week + $100 Flex Bucks
     - Price: $1,902.50/semester
     - Good for students with lighter dining needs

  4. 7 Meals/Week + $550 Flex Bucks
     - Price: $2,225.50/semester
     - Ideal for students who eat off-campus often but want on-campus convenience

EMPLOYEE MEAL PLANS
--------------------
Available to FAU faculty and staff.

OWL BUCKS
----------
- Prepaid declining balance account linked to the OWL Card
- Accepted at all on-campus dining locations, convenience stores, and the bookstore
- Anyone with an OWL Card can open an Owl Bucks account
- Check balance on receipts or via the GET app

OWL CARD
---------
All students are required to have an OWL Card, which serves as:
- Official photo ID
- Meal plan access card
- Library card
- Residence hall key
- Ticket to many FAU events (including sporting events)
- Owl Bucks debit card (NOT a regular banking debit card)

CHANGING YOUR MEAL PLAN
------------------------
- Students may change their meal plan once per semester, within 10 business days of the semester start
- Submit a meal plan change form online (through the FAU portal)
- Jupiter campus students: email Bizservices@fau.edu

DIETARY ACCOMMODATIONS
------------------------
- Students with food allergies, intolerances, or medical conditions can work directly with
  Dining Services to request accommodations within the Atlantic Dining Hall
- Students with severe allergies or disabilities that prevent safe use of the dining hall may
  apply for a Meal Plan Exemption (requires medical documentation)
- Vegan, vegetarian, and gluten-free options are available

JUPITER CAMPUS DINING
-----------------------
- Meal plans required for all Jupiter/Wilkes Honors College residents
- 19-Meal Plan: $2,246.00/semester (required for first-year students)
- 14-Meal Plan: $2,114.00/semester
- Managed by Business Services; visit dineoncampus.com/faujupiter for menus
